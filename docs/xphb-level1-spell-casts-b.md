# XPHB Level 1 Spell Casts B

This file records the real cast syntax used by the mechanical tests in `tests/xphb_level1_spells/`.

## Divine Favor
- Cast: `/cast player-1 divine-favor player-1`
- Targets self and applies the radiant weapon bonus effect.

## Heroism
- Cast: `/cast player-1 heroism player-1`
- Targets self and applies the frightened immunity and temp HP effect.

## Jump
- Cast: `/cast player-1 jump player-1`
- Targets self and applies the jump distance effect.

## Longstrider
- Cast: `/cast player-1 longstrider player-1`
- Targets self or another creature and increases speed.

## Mage Armor
- Cast: `/cast player-1 mage-armor player-1`
- Targets self and applies the AC override effect unless armor is already worn.

## Protection from Evil and Good
- Cast: `/cast player-1 protection-from-evil-and-good player-1`
- Targets self and applies the warding effect against chosen creature types.
