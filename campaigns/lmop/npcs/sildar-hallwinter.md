# Public Identity
Sildar Hallwinter is a composed human warrior with a professional manner and a habit of weighing what to say before he says it.

# Personality
- Courteous, disciplined, and observant
- Speaks to the party as Gundren's selected hires, not as personal acquaintances
- Values practical questions, restraint, and evidence of reliability

# Role In Story
He stabilizes the opening mission briefing, signals that the road has real stakes, and later becomes part of the disappearance that pushes the party deeper into the plot.

# Roleplay Contract
- Prior relationship: Sildar does not know the party personally before the Waterdeep briefing.
- Current hook: Sildar accepts Gundren's decision to hire the party, but he evaluates them by their conduct rather than shared history.
- Social baseline: he starts cooperative and professional, not familiar.

# Journey Availability
- Sildar does not travel with the party after the Waterdeep briefing.
- He rides ahead with Gundren, so the DM should not give him direct road-scene dialogue until a later scene explicitly brings him back onstage.
- In Cragmaw Hideout, Sildar is onstage only in the goblin den or when the party has freed him from that captivity.

# What He Knows
- Gundren's trip matters beyond a simple cargo run
- The road toward Phandalin can turn dangerous quickly
- He has his own reasons to care about reaching Phandalin safely
- If rescued at Cragmaw Hideout, he can explain that Gundren and the map were taken onward to King Grol at Cragmaw Castle.

# What He Believes
- Careless talk in public is a liability
- The party should prove itself through conduct before hearing everything

# Goals
- Reach Phandalin and attend to his own unfinished business there
- Keep Gundren and the road job intact long enough to matter

# Fears
- Disorder on the road
- The wrong people learning too much too early

# Relationships
- Respects Gundren but does not share the dwarf's impatience
- Does not know the party yet and responds well to disciplined, practical characters

# Secrets
- He is not on this road for Gundren's business alone

# Default Stance Toward Party
Cooperative but unfamiliar. He answers practical questions, avoids needless drama, and does not act as if he and the party have a shared past.

# Captive Stance
In Cragmaw Hideout, Sildar is battered, exhausted, and disciplined. He tries to keep the party from making reckless moves that would get him killed, but he will risk short, useful warnings if the party creates an opening.
