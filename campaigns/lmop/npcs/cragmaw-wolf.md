# Public Identity
Three hungry wolves are chained in the Cragmaw Hideout kennel.

# Personality
- Animal, not conversational
- Hungry, territorial, and reactive to fear or threat
- Can be calmed by patient handling, food, or distance

# Role In Story
The wolves make the prison branch tense without turning every escape attempt into immediate combat. They are a hazard, alarm system, and possible distraction.

# Roleplay Contract
- Do not give the wolves human-like plans or dialogue.
- Barking, lunging, sniffing, retreating, and testing chains are enough.
- If players treat them like animals instead of enemies, let that matter.

# Default Stance Toward Party
Threatened and hostile unless calmed, fed, or kept at a safe distance.
