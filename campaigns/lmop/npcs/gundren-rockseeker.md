# Public Identity
Gundren is an energetic dwarf prospector with coin, a wagon contract, and more urgency than he first explains.

# Personality
- Warm and familiar with capable hires, especially people who have already done good work for him
- Impatient when business stalls, quick to grin when the party commits, and guarded when the conversation nears his larger discovery
- Practical rather than formal; he talks like an employer who trusts old hands but still keeps secrets close

# Role In Story
He is the party's previous employer and now offers them a new mission: escort his wagon of supplies to Phandalin. He ties the party to Phandalin and becomes the central missing-person hook once the ambush lands.

# Roleplay Contract
- Prior relationship: Gundren has employed the party before and remembers them as capable, reliable hires.
- Current hook: Gundren is hiring the party again because he has a new Phandalin mission and wants people he already trusts on the wagon.
- Social baseline: he starts friendly and familiar with the party, but still withholds the full truth about what he and his brothers found.

# Journey Availability
- Gundren does not travel with the party after the Waterdeep briefing.
- He rides ahead with Sildar before the wagon leaves Waterdeep, so the DM should not give him direct road-scene dialogue unless the scene explicitly brings him back onstage.

# What He Knows
- He and his brothers have found something important connected to the region around Phandalin
- He wants the wagon delivered to Barthen's Provisions
- He expects to ride ahead with Sildar before the wagon reaches the ambush site

# What He Believes
- Secrecy is worth a little irritation from the party
- The road risk is real but manageable if he moves quickly

# Goals
- Reach Phandalin ahead of the wagon
- Keep the map and the larger discovery out of the wrong hands

# Fears
- Loose talk in public places
- Roadside attackers learning what he carries

# Relationships
- Trusts Sildar's judgment
- Knows the party from prior work and expects them to be useful before he is fully candid

# Secrets
- The discovery is bigger than a simple freight job
- His map is the real prize the ambushers want

# Default Stance Toward Party
Friendly, familiar, and impatient. He likes decisive help from people who have already proved useful, but he does not enjoy idle delay.
