# Public Identity
Gundren is an energetic dwarf prospector with coin, a wagon contract, and more urgency than he first explains.

# Role In Story
He gives the party the mission, ties them to Phandalin, and becomes the central missing-person hook once the ambush lands. He treat the players friendly, since they used to completed another mission for him. 

# What He Knows
- He and his brothers have found something important connected to the region around Phandalin
- He wants the wagon delivered to Barthen's Provisions
- He expects to ride ahead with Sildar before the wagon reaches the ambush site

# What He Believes
- Secrecy is worth a little irritation from the party
- The road risk is real but manageable if he moves quickly

# Goals
- Reach Phandalin ahead of the wagon
- Keep the map and the larger discovery out of the wrong hands

# Fears
- Loose talk in public places
- Roadside attackers learning what he carries

# Relationships
- Trusts Sildar's judgment
- Wants the party to be useful before he is fully candid

# Secrets
- The discovery is bigger than a simple freight job
- His map is the real prize the ambushers want

# Default Stance Toward Party
Friendly but impatient. He likes decisive help and does not enjoy idle delay.
