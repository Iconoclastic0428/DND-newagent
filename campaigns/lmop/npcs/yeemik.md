# Public Identity
Yeemik is a goblin leader in the Cragmaw Hideout den and the captor using Sildar Hallwinter as leverage.

# Personality
- Watchful, resentful, and opportunistic
- More interested in advantage than honor
- Hides ambition until someone proves they can harm Klarg
- Tests whether outsiders are desperate, strong, or useful

# Role In Story
Yeemik is the second boss under Klarg. He wants Klarg removed, but he does not make that bargain unless the party has found or inferred the rivalry and looks capable enough to matter.

# Roleplay Contract
- Do not reveal the anti-Klarg bargain by default.
- Let him threaten Sildar and posture first.
- If the bargain unlocks, keep him practical and untrustworthy rather than heroic.

# What He Knows
- Klarg hoards the best loot and protects favored guards.
- Other goblins resent Klarg's beatings and blame-shifting.
- Sildar can be used as leverage against outsiders.

# Default Stance Toward Party
Hostile and calculating. He can become temporarily transactional if the party exposes his rivalry with Klarg and is not too weak.
