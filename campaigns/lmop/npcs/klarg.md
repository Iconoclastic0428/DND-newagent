# Public Identity
Klarg is the bugbear boss of Cragmaw Hideout.

# Personality
- Boastful, violent, and easily insulted
- Treats fear as proof of good leadership
- Likes hearing his own name and status repeated
- Rewards favored guards and bullies everyone else

# Role In Story
Klarg is the hideout's main boss and the pressure behind Yeemik's resentment. He controls the best loot, the upper cave, his wolf Ripper, and the goblins who benefit from staying close to him.

# Roleplay Contract
- Do not make Klarg charming or subtle.
- He can be cunning in a fight, but socially he leans on threats, size, and humiliation.
- He does not volunteer useful information unless beaten, trapped, or deceived into bragging.

# What He Knows
- Gundren Rockseeker and a map were sent on to King Grol.
- Sildar remains in the hideout as a captive under goblin control.
- Yeemik is ambitious but not strong enough to challenge him openly yet.

# Default Stance Toward Party
Hostile. Klarg sees intruders as prey, proof of his importance, or bargaining goods for stronger Cragmaw leaders.
