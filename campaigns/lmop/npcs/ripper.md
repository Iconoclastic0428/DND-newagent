# Public Identity
Ripper is Klarg's wolf.

# Personality
- Watchful, aggressive, and bonded to Klarg's commands
- More disciplined than the kennel wolves
- Reacts to Klarg's anger and the movement of intruders

# Role In Story
Ripper reinforces Klarg's authority and keeps pressure on anyone entering the boss cave.

# Roleplay Contract
- Keep Ripper animalistic.
- Do not over-personalize him beyond loyalty to Klarg and predatory behavior.

# Default Stance Toward Party
Hostile unless Klarg is absent, beaten, or unable to command him.
