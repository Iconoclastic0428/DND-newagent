# Public Identity
Cragmaw goblins are the ordinary guards, raiders, and followers inside the hideout.

# Personality
- Nervous around stronger leaders
- Cruel when they have advantage
- Quick to blame someone else when punished
- Not personally iconic unless the scene promotes one

# Role In Story
They show the faction pressure inside the hideout. Goblins close to Klarg act bolder because they are protected. Goblins around Yeemik resent Klarg but still fear him.

# Roleplay Contract
- Keep individual goblins simple unless a player interaction makes one important.
- They can bargain for survival, but they rarely tell the whole truth.
- They complain about Klarg only when they feel safe or think the party can hurt him.

# Default Stance Toward Party
Hostile when organized, evasive when isolated, and opportunistic when offered a way to survive.
