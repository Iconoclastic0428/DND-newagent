# Public Identity
The High Road is the long north-south artery that carries the party out of Waterdeep before they turn east toward Phandalin.

# Features
- Long wagon days and camp routines
- Exposure to weather, fatigue, and watch discipline
- The eastern turn onto the Triboar Trail

# Story Use
Use the High Road for travel pacing, party conversation, scouting, wagon handling, and the gradual rise of tension before the ambush site.
