# Public Identity
Cragmaw Hideout is the goblin lair implied by the trail clues.

# Story Use
It becomes relevant after the ambush trail is followed or after the party is captured by the ambushers.

# Keyed Areas For This Slice
- Cave mouth: a stream exits the cave, with brush and rock giving goblins places to watch the approach.
- Wolf kennel: three wolves are chained near the entrance. They attack strangers unless calmed, distracted, or fed.
- Goblin den: Sildar Hallwinter is held here at the mercy of Yeemik and his followers.
- Twin pools and upper cave: Klarg controls the hideout from the upper cave with his favored guards and wolf.

# Faction Pressure
Klarg is the named boss of the hideout. He is violent, boastful, and backed by the goblins who guard him directly. Yeemik is the second boss in the goblin den and wants Klarg removed, but he does not reveal that weakness casually. Goblins aligned with Yeemik resent Klarg because his favored guards get better treatment while the rest are bullied, beaten, and blamed.

# DM Boundaries
- The party may leave for Phandalin through regional travel; retain rescued NPCs, HP, inventory and unresolved Cragmaw leads.
- Use the wolf kennel capture branch only when the party is defeated and the capture condition is available.
- If every player character is unconscious and no capture or rescue condition is available, end the game.
