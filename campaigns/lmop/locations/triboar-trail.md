# Public Identity
The Triboar Trail is the eastward road toward Phandalin where the goblin ambush springs.

# Features
- Lower road bed with steep embankments
- Dense brush and treeline concealment
- Narrow lanes that make wagons and riders easy to trap

# Story Use
This location is where road travel tightens into danger, clues, and pursuit. It should feel rougher, narrower, and more exposed than the High Road.
