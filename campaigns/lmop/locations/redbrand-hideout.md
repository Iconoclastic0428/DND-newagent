# Public Summary
A dungeon below Tresendar Manor.

# Hidden DM Notes
- Cellar: A narrow landing overlooks a cellar with two stairways, supply barrels and a stone cistern.
- Barracks: Bunks and fresh provisions occupy this storage room.
- Trapped Hall: Dust covers the passage leading west to copper-clad doors.
- Tresendar Crypts: Three stone sarcophagi stand among carved columns and silent skeletal sentries.
- Prisoner Pens: Iron bars divide a long chamber into cells. Frightened captives wait inside.
- Armory: Weapon racks line the walls; red cloaks hang beside the entrance.
- Storeroom: Packing straw, stolen cargo and tools fill the northern end of the cavern.
- Crevasse: Two narrow wooden bridges span a deep, cold fissure. A voice presses into your thoughts from the shadows.
- Guard Barracks: Four crude beds fill a foul-smelling barracks; a frightened goblin cowers nearby.
- Common Room: Ale barrels and mismatched tables surround a noisy game of dice.
- Wizard's Workshop: Glass vessels bubble on a workbench. A rat watches from beneath the furniture.
- Glasstaff's Quarters: Red drapes frame a desk, bed and unlocked chest in a private chamber.
