# Public Identity
Waterdeep is the great city where this demo opens before the party takes Gundren's road job north.

# Features
- Busy taverns, contracts, and caravan yards
- Plenty of witnesses, noise, and social pressure
- A clear contrast with the isolation of the road that follows

# Story Use
Use Waterdeep to establish the job, the tone of Gundren and Sildar, and the party's first shared decisions before the wilderness takes over.
