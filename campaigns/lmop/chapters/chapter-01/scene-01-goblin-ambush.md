# Public Summary
After days on the road, the party reaches the Triboar Trail and finds the aftermath of violence just before goblin arrows fly from the brush above the road.

# Read Aloud
You've been on the Triboar Trail for roughly half a day when the road bends around a clutter of brush and steep banks. Two riderless horses wander near the track, nosing at spilled packs and torn gear. Dense thickets press close on both sides of the trail. Then the arrows start.

# Hidden DM Notes
This scene is grounded in the official ambush setup: two horses, scattered gear, steep embankments, dense brush, and an organized goblin attack tied to Gundren and Sildar's disappearance. If the players recognized the horses, surface that clearly before or during the ambush.

# Scene Goals
- Flip the story cleanly from travel into immediate danger
- Confirm that Gundren and Sildar have been intercepted
- Give the DM runtime a clear trigger for entering structured combat

# Important Actors
- Goblin ambushers
- Gundren Rockseeker
- Sildar Hallwinter

# Keyed Features
- Steep embankments and dense thickets
- Two riderless horses and scattered gear
- A narrow road lane that makes the party vulnerable

# Triggers
- If the party investigates the horses or scattered supplies, the ambush can spring
- If hostile intent becomes immediate, initiative should begin
- If the party captures a goblin afterward, the trail to the hideout becomes the next clue

# Likely Checks
- Perception to pick out the ambush line before the first arrows land
- Survival to read the goblin trail after the fight
- Medicine or Investigation to assess what happened to the riders

# Branching Outcomes
- Fight off the goblin ambushers
- Pull back and regroup under fire
- Secure the site first, then decide whether to pursue

# Secrets
- The goblins belong to the Cragmaw operation
- Gundren and the map were the real target
