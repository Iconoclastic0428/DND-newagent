# Public Summary
Klarg controls the upper cave with his wolf Ripper and favored goblin guards. Stolen supplies and a chest mark this as the boss's space.

# Read Aloud
The upper cave smells of smoke, wet fur, and stolen goods. Crates and sacks are piled under guard, while a hulking bugbear keeps the best space for himself. His wolf watches from the shadows with a low, patient growl.

# Hidden DM Notes
Klarg is the hideout's main boss. He is cruel, grandiose, and convinced that fear is the same thing as loyalty. His favored guards tolerate him because they benefit from his protection; other goblins hate him because he beats them, withholds loot, and shifts blame onto them.

# Klarg Behavior
- Speaks in a swollen, self-important way.
- Refers to his own strength, authority, and favored status often.
- Uses Ripper and loyal goblin guards to keep distance and pressure.
- Does not bargain from weakness unless badly cornered.

# Chest And Rivalry Clues
The chest or boss supplies can reveal that Klarg hoards the good loot, keeps favored guards comfortable, and punishes goblins outside his inner circle. Finding these clues should set `cragmaw_rivalry_clue_found=true` so the Yeemik bargain becomes available if the party is strong enough to matter.

# Tactical Pressure
- Klarg, Ripper, and loyal goblins should protect the boss's advantage.
- Yeemik's followers are not eager to die for Klarg.
- If the party exposes the rivalry, some goblins may hesitate rather than reinforce Klarg.
