# Public Summary
If the goblin ambush defeats the party and the capture condition is available, the party wakes in the wolf kennel of Cragmaw Hideout.

# Read Aloud
Cold stone presses against your back. Rope bites your wrists. Three wolves pace and pull at their chains, close enough that their breath huffs through the dark. Somewhere deeper in the cave, goblins argue over stolen gear.

# Hidden DM Notes
This is a survival branch, not an automatic game over. The goblins have dragged the defeated party to the hideout, stripped obvious valuables, and left them near the chained wolves because it is cruel and convenient. The wolves attack non-goblins who get too close unless calmed, fed, or otherwise managed.

# Wolf Behavior
- Three wolves are chained to iron rods in the kennel.
- They bark, lunge, and test the chains when frightened, hungry, or hurt.
- Animal Handling can calm them; offering food or avoiding direct threat should reduce the difficulty.
- A goblin or bugbear can release a wolf if the cave is fully alerted.

# Player Options
- Work loose from bonds
- Calm or distract the wolves
- Search the kennel for a way out
- Listen for goblin movements and Sildar's location

# Game End Boundary
If all player characters are unconscious and no capture or rescue condition applies, do not use this scene. The game ends instead.
