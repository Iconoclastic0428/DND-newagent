# Public Summary
The hidden trail ends at a cave mouth where a shallow stream runs out between brush, stones, and shadowed rock.

# Read Aloud
The goblin trail narrows into damp earth and stone. Ahead, a stream spills from a dark cave mouth. The air smells of wet fur, smoke, and old blood.

# Hidden DM Notes
This is the entry point to Cragmaw Hideout. Goblins use the brush and cave features to watch for intruders. Keep the pressure practical: the party can sneak, scout, rush, lure a guard, or withdraw to plan, or choose regional travel to Phandalin without erasing their unfinished rescue lead.

# Scene Goals
- Let the party choose an approach to the cave
- Foreshadow the wolf kennel and goblin voices deeper inside
- Keep Sildar and Gundren as urgent unresolved stakes

# Important Actors

- Three wolves in the kennel
- Sildar Hallwinter deeper in the cave
