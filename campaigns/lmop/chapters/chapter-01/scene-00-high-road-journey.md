# Public Summary
The party spends several days on the High Road with Gundren's wagon, then turns east onto the Triboar Trail toward Phandalin.

# Read Aloud
The city falls behind you. For days the High Road is wind, wheels, leather creaks, and the steady work of keeping a wagon moving. At last the route bends east, and the narrower Triboar Trail draws you toward rougher country and older dangers.

# Hidden DM Notes
This scene is the bridge into the official road sequence. The local official text establishes a few uneventful travel days, then a turn onto the Triboar Trail where danger is known but not yet immediate. 

# Scene Goals
- Let the players settle travel roles and party dynamics
- Surface ordinary road hazards and the feel of a longer trip
- Give the DM runtime room to call for travel-facing checks before the ambush

# Important Actors
- The party
- Gundren Rockseeker if he has not already ridden ahead in the current telling
- Sildar Hallwinter if he remains visible before riding ahead

# Keyed Features
- Wagon, draft animals, and cargo lashings
- Roadside camp routine
- The eastern turn that leads onto the Triboar Trail

# Triggers
- If the party discusses watches, marching order, or scouting, travel checks may be appropriate
- If the party accepts Gundren's plan to ride ahead with Sildar, move them offstage before the ambush
- Once the party has had enough road interaction, advance to the ambush scene

# Likely Checks
- Survival to read the road and weather
- Perception to notice tracks, broken brush, or unease before trouble. This should only be check once, and use this as the final result.

# Branching Outcomes
- Smooth travel with only conversation and minor road work
- Small moments of strain that build tension before the ambush
- Early suspicion that someone is watching the road

# Secrets
- Gundren and Sildar are meant to be ahead of the wagon by the time the ambush site is found
