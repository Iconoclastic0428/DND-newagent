# Public Summary
The fight is over and the road is littered with riderless horses, scattered gear, drag marks, and a fresh goblin trail that leaves the main road.

# Read-Aloud
The arrows are gone, but the silence feels wrong. Two battered horses stand near torn packs, and the brush north of the road is freshly broken. The trail does not point toward Phandalin; it vanishes into the wooded rise.

# Hidden DM Notes
The goblin tracks create an urgent rescue lead, but the party may instead continue to Phandalin. Use the regional travel commands to resolve that choice; arrival opens the authored town site. Preserve all rescue outcomes and unresolved leads.
