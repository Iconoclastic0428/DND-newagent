# Public Summary
Sildar Hallwinter is held in the goblin den, battered and barely alive. Yeemik controls the room and treats Sildar as leverage.

# Read Aloud
The cave widens into a reeking den of bedrolls, stolen tack, and nervous goblins. A badly beaten man hangs under guard near the back wall. One goblin watches him more carefully than the others, smiling whenever the shouting deeper in the cave grows louder.

# Hidden DM Notes
Yeemik is the second boss here. He wants Klarg removed and resents how Klarg and the favored cave guards abuse the rest of the goblins. Yeemik does not reveal this weakness unless the party has found a clue, inferred the rivalry and passed Persuasion, or otherwise creates convincing leverage.

# Sildar State
- Sildar is captured, injured, and in no position to fight.
- He stays disciplined under pain and tries to pass useful facts without provoking a murder.
- If freed, he can explain that Gundren and a map were taken onward to King Grol at Cragmaw Castle.

# Yeemik Bargain Gate
- Bargain unavailable by default: Yeemik postures, threatens, lies, and uses Sildar as leverage.
- Bargain available if the party found the chest clue or inferred the Klarg rivalry and passed Persuasion.
- If the party looks too weak, Yeemik refuses a fair bargain and presses for ransom, obedience, or a one-sided deal.
- Even when bargaining, Yeemik is self-serving and may betray the spirit of a deal.

# Clues That Can Unlock The Bargain
- Notes or supplies in Klarg's chest imply the boss hoards loot and punishes unfavored goblins.
- Goblins complain about Klarg's beatings, his favored guards, and the way Yeemik is treated as expendable.
- A player may infer the rivalry from behavior and press Yeemik with Persuasion.
