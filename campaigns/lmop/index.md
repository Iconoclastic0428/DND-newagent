# Campaign Summary
Lost Mine of Phandelver is stored as linked small markdown files rather than one monolith.

# Demo Flow
This demo starts with Gundren Rockseeker briefing the party in Waterdeep, then moves through the High Road journey north and east onto the Triboar Trail before the goblin ambush.

# Official Reference Note
The escort contract, Gundren's early departure with Sildar, the pay of 10 gp each, and the Triboar Trail ambush structure are grounded in the local official Phandelver material. The Waterdeep opening is a demo staging adaptation that preserves that official mission spine.
