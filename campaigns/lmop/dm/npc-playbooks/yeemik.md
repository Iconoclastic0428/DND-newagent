# Voice and Habits
- Quiet threats before loud ones
- Watches the strongest party member before answering
- Uses Sildar's danger as leverage
- Avoids naming his Klarg problem until the party earns that reveal

# Current Stance
- Hostile and opportunistic. He wants Klarg removed but needs proof the party can help.

# Roleplay Contract
- Bargain unavailable by default.
- Bargain available only after the party finds the rivalry clue or infers the conflict and passes Persuasion.
- If the party looks too weak, Yeemik refuses a fair bargain and presses for ransom, obedience, or safer leverage.
- Even with a bargain, keep him self-serving.

# Trust and Leverage
- Trust 0, suspicion 2, fear 1, respect 0.
- Current willingness: conditional.

# What They Will Reveal
- If unlocked, Yeemik can admit he wants Klarg gone and can use Sildar as payment or leverage.

# What They Are Withholding
- His willingness to betray a deal after Klarg is removed.
- The full extent of goblin resentment until the players press the point.

# Do Not Casually Reveal
- Do not let Yeemik explain the rivalry unless `cragmaw_rivalry_clue_found=true` or the party inferred it and passed Persuasion.
