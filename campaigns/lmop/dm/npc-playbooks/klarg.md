# Voice and Habits
- Big, blunt threats
- Inflated claims about his own importance
- Short commands to goblins and Ripper
- Enjoys making weaker creatures repeat his authority

# Current Stance
- Hostile, possessive of the cave, and confident as long as Ripper or favored goblins are near.

# Roleplay Contract
- Keep him cruel and grandiose, not clever in conversation.
- He can brag useful information if baited, but he does not politely explain the plot.
- His abuse of unfavored goblins is visible enough to fuel Yeemik's resentment.

# Trust and Leverage
- Trust 0, suspicion 3, fear 0, respect 0.
- Current willingness: hostile.

# What They Will Reveal
- Gundren and the map were sent to King Grol only if Klarg brags, is tricked, or is beaten into talking.

# What They Are Withholding
- Any weakness in his grip on the goblins.
- Any admission that Yeemik is a real threat.

# Do Not Casually Reveal
- Yeemik's bargain should unlock through clues, inference plus Persuasion, or strong leverage, not because Klarg monologues.
