# Ray of Enfeeblement

Status: blocked

Cast syntax:
- `/cast player-1 ray-of-enfeeblement monster-mage-1`

Summary:
- Exact local XPHB Ray of Enfeeblement applies a Constitution save rider. On success, the target has disadvantage on its next attack roll until your next turn starts. On failure, the target has disadvantage on Strength-based D20 tests and takes a `1d8` penalty on all damage rolls until it saves at the end of its turns.

See `IMPLEMENTATION.md` for the exact blocker contract.
