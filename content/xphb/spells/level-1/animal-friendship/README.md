# Animal Friendship

XPHB level-1 story-adjudicated spell rollout entry.

Cast syntax: `/cast <actor-id> animal-friendship --description <requested use>`

Local XPHB notes:
- 1 action, 30-foot point, visible beast, Wisdom save.
- On failure, the beast is charmed for 24 hours.
- If you or your allies deal damage to the target, the spell ends.
- Higher-level slots add one additional beast target per slot above 1st.
